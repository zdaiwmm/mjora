ChinesePlaceNames.json is derived from GeoNames geographical data:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/CN.zip
https://www.geonames.org/
Accessed 2026-11-17. GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This application extracts populated places (500+ inhabitants or administrative
seats) and administrative divisions, normalizes Latin name aliases, and uses
Simplified Chinese alternate names. For ambiguous aliases the largest populated
place is preferred. Original server metadata is preserved.
